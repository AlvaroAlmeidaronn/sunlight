# Code of Conduct

Sunlight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and leave out
harassment, insults and personal attacks of any kind.

Report unacceptable behaviour to d@spacefarm.digital. Every report is read and kept confidential, and the
maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block people who don't follow this code.
